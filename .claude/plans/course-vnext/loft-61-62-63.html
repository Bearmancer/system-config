Russia publishes no deserter count. The video reads the silence as fear of handing the West a propaganda win; nothing on record states that motive.

Courts have convicted about 28,000 soldiers for leaving their units since the invasion began (Meduza 2026). That tally covers only soldiers who were caught, so it is a floor. A UN estimate puts desertions at over 50,000 since 2022, close to double the convictions (Novaya Gazeta 2025). The 16,000 and 13,500 figures that circulate in this debate are cumulative and all-service counts, not desertions (Novaya Gazeta 2025).

The video’s “70,000-plus” is a projection of voluntary departures by the end of 2025, not a count (Frontelligence Insight 2025). Whether that is “almost 10 percent” of the force depends on which deployed total is used. On the Ukrainian side, documented unauthorized absence is 140,000 to 180,000, below the video’s 200,000 (Frontelligence Insight 2025).

Diagram colour rule

Afghanistan, chapter 5. Rule: colour is the relationship type (attack, alliance, rift), the same colour whoever the actor, like kinship types in a family tree. The arrow runs from actor to target. All lines are solid. An edge that is not a recurring type uses the neutral colour. A label on a line names that one event: “NRF raids north” is one attack, by the NRF.

Node and line labels are from the live page. Layout is computed: nodes are ordered so lines never cross, line ends are spread along each box edge, and labels slide along their line until they clear every box and label.
