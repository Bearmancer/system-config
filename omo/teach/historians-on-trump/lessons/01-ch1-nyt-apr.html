The double witness holds: the report pointers and the cast table beside them agree on 35 historians and 22 actions, so the December 36 must never be swapped into this round, a misreading corrected at the start.

"whether 22 Trump actions had precedent"

That quoted line is the instrument speaking, not the historians rendering a verdict, and the distinction is confirmed as method-asked framing [C4][O1]. Readers who recall one sweeping unprecedented label for the round carry a memory the record does not support; the teaching here separates the question posed from the answers sorted.

"no precedent as asked / modern-era precedent / different degree-kind / not uncommon"

The answers fell into those four buckets rather than one verdict, a split confirmed against the frozen record with the claim-graph lock holding it as partial and qualified [C4][O1]. No bucket may be quoted alone as the round finding; the four-way shape is the finding.

Three asked-about items, the Fed firing, the drug-boats action and the pardons, travel inside this round as framing only, and any fact claim built on them is barred by the annex rule [C4][O1]. The full-page body beyond the frozen BrightData capture stays unverified, so this lesson teaches the asked-framing and the split, and stops with the frozen page described in the April write-up.
