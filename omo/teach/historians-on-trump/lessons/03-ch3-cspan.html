3. Narrative

The report places Trump 41st with 312 points from 142 raters on a 1 to 10 scale across ten equally weighted traits, and that placement is confirmed against the frozen survey record with double witness. Report pointers, subgraph text, manifest and synthesis agree on 41st and 312 alike; the vague 140 and the recalled 300 are corrected to 142 and 312, and the numerals below travel only in their canonical form.

4. Machinery

The instrument asks each rater to grade every president 1 to 10 on ten traits, then weights the traits equally and sums to the overall score. That design makes 312 a within-series total only; cross-series reading follows the convergence, never the raw points.

Page discipline keeps the citation honest: weights cite methodology, rank and score cite the overall page, and the anchor story cites its own 2018 record. One page per half, every retelling.
