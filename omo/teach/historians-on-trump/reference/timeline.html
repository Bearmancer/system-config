How to read this page

Every entry is one date, one line of event, one colour tag for its kind, and a status in plain words. Verified means the placement or count was checked against the frozen record. Partial means the record supports part and corrects part. Attributed means a voice said it on record and it is taught as who-said-it, never as fact. Qualified means the fact travels only beside its qualifier. Framing-only means the item is named but barred from fact teaching. Colour carries kind only, never status. Where the record corrects the source, the entry sits at the corrected value and says so.

Legend — seven kinds, one colour each

  • survey round NYT asked-framing instruments and their rounds (Apr 30 2025, Dec 22 2025).
  • ranking instrument C-SPAN 2021, PGP 2024, Siena 2022 — method, field window, and placement.
  • historian voice On-record claims by named historians (Meacham, Brinkley, Riley, Perry, frames).
  • counter-precedent Historical counters bounding the denial claim (Jackson 1824, Nixon 1960, Gore 2000).
  • context record Hosts, archives, and pages framing a voice without carrying it.
  • annex framing A1–A3 items held as NYT-asked framing only, never fact.
  • method freeze Corrections, pending instruments, gaps, and the 2026-09-21 freeze line.

Status guide, written out on every entry: "verified — …" (frozen record confirms) · "partial — …" (split verdict, part corrected) · "attributed — …" (voice says it; record has not made it fact) · "qualified — …" (fact travels only with its qualifier) · "framing-only — …" (named, barred from fact teaching). Kind meanings hold steady as the page grows; old entries are never recoloured.
