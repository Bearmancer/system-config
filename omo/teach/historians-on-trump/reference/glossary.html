Verdict and attribution terms

  • Asked-framing — the instrument posed precedent as a question ("whether 22 Trump actions had precedent"); historians sorted answers into buckets. Method line, never a verdict. ch1 lesson.
  • Degree-kind qualifier — denial claims hold only as same type at sharply different scale (Meacham). Absolute-first versions are corrected to degree-kind; the qualifier travels beside every citation. ch5 lesson.
  • Attribution only — voice-says standing: Brinkley no-firewall, Riley steroids, and the five ch7 frames are taught as who said it, where, with what qualifier — never as fact. ch6 lesson; ch7 lesson.
  • Supported-as-attribution — quote text verified against the frozen record while the underlying claim stays voice-grade (Riley steroids, Perry braggadocio wording). ch6 lesson.
  • Framing-only (annex rule) — "cite NYT-asked framing only … with no fact claim". A1–A3 stay named but inert: never carried into any verdict sentence. ch8 lesson.
  • Unverified / unfindable — full-page body beyond frozen captures, Beschloss long-form, Goodwin Morning Joe transcript, MTP video-only extensions: absent from the transcript record, left out of teaching. ch8 lesson.
