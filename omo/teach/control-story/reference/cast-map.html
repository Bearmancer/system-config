Roster — Directors and Bureau command

  • Jesse Faden (ch1) — incoming Director searching for her brother; binds the Service Weapon in Trench's office and the Board names her Director. Earns the title across the game and accepts the chair for real in the ending. ch1 lesson; plot in ch2; ending in ch13.
  • Dylan Faden (ch2) — Jesse's brother; embraced the Hiss and opened its door. Raised as P6, judged a lost cause, held in the Panopticon; cleansed into a coma when Jesse shuts the projector. Whether he ever wakes stays unverified. ch2 lesson; candidates in ch10; ending in ch13.
  • Zachariah Trench (ch1) — dead Director whose gun starts everything; speaks from the Hotline after death. Ordered the Ordinary response and kept a slide, which became the Hiss road in; fell to the Hiss first. ch1 lesson; history in ch4.
  • Theodore Ash (ch4) — first Director; set the Bureau's early shape. ch4 lesson.
  • Broderick Northmoor (ch4) — later Director; grew too powerful and was contained. The yardstick Dylan was measured against. ch4 lesson; returns in ch10.
  • Helen Marshall (ch2) — Head of Operations; holds the Research Sector, then her sector falls first. Her damage to the Nail started the Foundation leak. ch2 lesson; Foundation in ch3.
  • Emily Pope (ch2) — researcher who decodes the Hiss for Jesse; catalogues the caves from above and continues Darling's research after he is gone. Stays to run the long cleanup. ch2 lesson; containment in ch7; ending in ch13.
  • Frederick Langston (ch7) — Panopticon supervisor chasing runaways. ch7 lesson.

Roster — research

  • Casper Darling (ch1; focus ch11) — Head of Research; speaks only through recordings Jesse finds after he is gone. Led the expeditions that found Hedron, built the HRA ward from its resonance, mapped the Hiss route in. His fate after the Hedron chamber stays unrecorded — unverified. ch1 lesson; focus in ch11.

Roster — entities and powers

  • The Board (ch1; focus ch5) — unseen entities speaking through the inverted pyramid; they name each Director and Jesse alone parses them. Darling's hypothesis limits them to the pyramid, not the whole Plane. Their origins are never established anywhere in the canon cited — unverified. ch1 lesson; focus in ch5.
  • Polaris / Hedron (ch2; Hedron named ch11) — resonance entity found in Slidescape-36; Jesse calls it Polaris. Darling's expeditions found the polyhedron organism and dubbed it Hedron; after its death Polaris resurfaces inside Jesse. ch2 lesson; Hiss in ch8; naming in ch11.
  • The Hiss (ch1; focus ch8) — hostile resonance that seizes minds; entered through Trench's kept slide, not as an outside attack. Beaten back in the ending, not beaten; the lockdown holds. ch1 lesson; focus in ch8; ending in ch13.
  • The Former (ch3) — exiled Board member building an opposition; offers Jesse a second path and a second power. ch3 lesson; exile in ch5.
  • Ahti (ch1; focus ch12) — janitor who takes Jesse on as assistant; guides repairs and grants maze passage with the cassette player, crossing Trench's binding at will. His nature is never established — god, entity, or the House itself all unverified. ch1 lesson; focus in ch12.
