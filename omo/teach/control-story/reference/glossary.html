People and powers

  • Jesse Faden — incoming Director; binds the Service Weapon and takes the chair. ch1; ch13.
  • Dylan Faden — Jesse's brother; P6, embraced the Hiss, cleansed into a coma. Whether he wakes stays unverified. ch2; ch10.
  • Zachariah Trench — late Director; speaks from the Hotline after death; kept the slide that let the Hiss in. ch1; ch4.
  • Helen Marshall — Head of Operations; her Nail damage starts the Foundation leak. ch2; ch3.
  • Emily Pope — Bureau researcher; decodes the Hiss, continues Darling's work, stays for the cleanup. ch2; ch7.
  • Casper Darling — Head of Research; speaks only through recordings. Found Hedron, built the HRAs. His fate after the Hedron chamber stays unrecorded — unverified. ch11.
  • Board — unseen entities speaking through the pyramid; they name each Director. Their origins are never established — unverified. ch5.
  • Ahti — janitor; helper, guide, and open question. His nature is never established — unverified. ch12.
  • The Former — exiled Board member building an opposition; offers Jesse a second power. ch3; ch5.
  • Alan Wake — missing writer signaling from the Dark Place. Whether he authored any of Jesse's story stays unverified. ch9.

Resonance and threats

  • Hedron / Polaris — resonance entity found in Slidescape-36; Jesse calls it Polaris. Darling's find, source of HRA resonance; after its death Polaris resurfaces inside Jesse. ch8; ch11.
  • Hiss — hostile resonance that seizes minds; entered through the Slide Projector. Beaten back, not beaten. ch8.
  • HRA — Hedron Resonance Amplifier; Darling-built device that blocks the Hiss. ch7; ch11.
  • Dark Place — the depth Wake signals from; the sealed sector's Bright Falls files point at it. ch9.
  • Third Thing — Hartman plus Darkness plus Hiss merged into something worse. ch9.

Objects and programs

  • Altered World Event (AWE) — a breach by paranatural forces; the Bureau investigates and covers up each one. ch6.
  • Astral Plane — white void of obelisks and an inverted pyramid; site of power rituals and the Board. ch5.
  • Object of Power — paranatural item bound through Astral ritual; grants abilities. ch2.
  • Oldest House — shifting Bureau headquarters in Manhattan. ch1.
  • Panopticon — Containment Sector prison for Altered Items. ch7.
  • Prime Candidate Program — Bureau search for Director successors; Dylan is P6, Jesse P7. ch10.
  • Service Weapon — Director-bound gun; choosing it makes Jesse Director. ch1.
  • Slide Projector — Ordinary Object of Power; its slides open doors to other dimensions. ch6.
  • Slidescape-36 — surviving slide dimension; home of Hedron and the Hiss route in. ch6.
  • Ashtray Maze — Trench-bound shifting labyrinth; Ahti crosses it at will and lends Jesse passage. ch12.

Places

  • Foundation / The Nail — caves beneath the Maintenance Sector; the Nail holds the Plane back until Marshall's damage. ch3.
  • Ordinary — town erased by the dump-projector AWE; the siblings' origin. Burned-slide tallies differ, given unsettled. ch6.
  • Containment Sector — Panopticon sector; its cells once held Dylan. ch7.
  • Investigations Sector — sealed sector holding the Bright Falls files; unsealed on Wake's call. ch9.
