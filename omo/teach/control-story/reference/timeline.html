How to read this page

Every entry is one chapter beat plus a colour tag for its kind, and a status in plain words. Confirmed means the canon record supports the beat. Corrected means a first impression is overturned by the canon record. Unverified means the beat is stated but the canon cited here never establishes it — taught as gap, never as fact. Kind meanings hold steady as the page grows; old entries are never recoloured.

Legend — seven kinds, one colour each

  • arrival Jesse walks into the House and takes the chair.
  • bureau history Directors, discovery, and the programs that predate Jesse.
  • ruling power The Board, the Former, and the pyramid they speak through.
  • buried past Ordinary, containment, the Hiss, and the candidates.
  • crossover Bright Falls entries reaching into the House.
  • ending The projector goes dark and the chair is taken.
  • held gap Named unknowns the course refuses to fill.

Status guide, written out on every entry: "confirmed — …" (canon supports it) · "corrected — …" (first impression overturned) · "unverified — …" (stated but never established; taught as gap).
