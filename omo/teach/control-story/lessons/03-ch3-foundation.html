3. Narrative

Jesse enters through the Restricted Area and asks the obvious question:

"Is this still a part of the Oldest House? It feels much older."

The line is confirmed on the Foundation location page, which places the caves beneath the Maintenance Sector and notes the red sand assayed as nothing stranger than iron oxide.

The leak itself is confirmed there too. Marshall damaged the Nail during the Hiss invasion, the Plane bled through permanently, and Jesse's repair stops the spread without undoing what already crossed over.

The Former's history arrives as a grudge:

"Blamed for something by the Board."

The exile story is confirmed in outline, while this exact wording stays unverified outside the video's captions. The base-game picture of a united command is corrected here: Director and Board openly disagree over the Nail, and the Foundation review notes Jesse and the Board end the DLC at odds.

What became of Marshall in the caves stays unverified in this pass; the video moves on without settling it, and so does this lesson.

4. Machinery

The Nail works as a lid. Everything Jesse does below is lid maintenance: four keys, one monolith, and two new crystal abilities granted along the way.

The Former's gift matters because it is the first power Jesse holds that the Board did not approve.
