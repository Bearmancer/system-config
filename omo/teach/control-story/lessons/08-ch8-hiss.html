3. Narrative

The invasion looks external until the tapes play. The first impression of an outside attack is corrected by the invasion record: Trench, already under influence, worked the projector himself, so the door opened from inside.

What came through announces itself endlessly:

"You are a worm through time."

The sentence is confirmed as the Hiss incantation, repeated by the possessed and by Trench on the Hotline alike.

The counter-frequency has a name and a shape. Darling's expeditions into the surviving slide dimension found a polyhedron organism he dubbed Hedron, the source of HRA resonance. That find is confirmed in the plot record, and so is the price: when the Hiss destroys Hedron, Jesse's mind nearly follows, until she rediscovers Polaris within herself. Dylan's boast that the Hiss set him free stays unverified as anything but his own allegiance, and the Hiss page keeps the entity's own origins blank.

4. Machinery

Hiss mechanics are resonance mechanics. Exposure converts, HRAs jam the signal, and Hedron out-sang the invasion until it died.

Polaris inside Jesse is the last transmitter standing, which is why the ending can turn on an inner voice rather than a bigger gun.
