3. Narrative

The sector's purpose is confirmed on the Containment Sector page: the Panopticon stores Altered Items and Objects of Power, including the Arctic Queen refrigerator and the Benicoff TV, behind cells built for things that cannot be killed.

Dylan's place in that system is a cell of his own:

"Relocated to a containment cell in the Panopticon, considered a lost cause."

The P6 cell and the lost-cause judgment are confirmed on Dylan's page, which records the agent killings that put him there after Darling's protection ran out.

Dylan's own account of his loyalties is blunter:

"I welcomed the Hiss. I let it in. The Hiss set me free."

The embrace is confirmed as his stated position, while his claim that Polaris uses Jesse stays unverified: it reads as anger at abandonment rather than evidence.

4. Machinery

Containment runs on two tools. Cells hold objects, and HRAs hold minds, since the Hiss takes people rather than things.

Darling's amplifiers only work while Hedron sings, which ties this sector's safety to a god in another chapter.
