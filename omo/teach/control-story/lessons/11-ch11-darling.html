3. Narrative

Darling never appears alive; he presents. His tapes guide Jesse through Dimensional Research to the chamber, and the find they describe is:

"A polyhedron-shaped organism he coined the Hedron."

The naming is confirmed in the wiki plot record: several expeditions into the one surviving slide dimension, a living source of HRA resonance, and a name Darling invented on the spot.

The assumption that these presentations are live briefings is corrected by their placement. They are recordings Jesse finds after Darling is already gone, which is why every tape feels one step behind the disaster.

His end is the gap this lesson refuses to fill. Whether he died, transformed, or walked somewhere unmappable is unverified across his page and every file cited here. No tape says where he went, and this pass invents nothing.

4. Machinery

Darling's machine is the presentation itself: cheerful videos that convert apocalypse into procedure, one threshold at a time.

The HRAs are his legacy hardware. Every agent Jesse meets alive wears his work.
