3. Narrative

The climax runs through the Astral Plane: Dylan tries to hand the Board's seat to the Hiss, Jesse pulls him back, and the projector goes dark. That sequence is confirmed in the ending breakdown, which also confirms the price and the aftermath: Dylan comatose, Jesse seated, and the lockdown still in force.

The false defeat first: Jesse falls, the credits roll early, and she wakes a bureaucrat in a Hiss dream. The despair is corrected by the Oceanview return, where Polaris turns out to have ridden inside her all along. The dream-office beat is confirmed in the same breakdown.

The last word belongs to the music:

"Take Control."

The Old Gods of Asgard track that carries the maze run shares its name with the final mission, both confirmed in the mission list and the collectible files. Whether Dylan ever wakes stays unverified in the base game, and the expansions pick up the chair, not the coma.

4. Machinery

The ending machine is a fake-out with a receipt. The false credits spend Jesse's defeat, then the motel scene refunds it with interest.

What remains is maintenance: a Director, a lockdown, and a House still humming with the unconverted.
