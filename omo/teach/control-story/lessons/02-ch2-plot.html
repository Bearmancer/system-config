3. Narrative

Jesse arrives pulled by Polaris, finds Trench dead of a gunshot, and lifts the Service Weapon. The Astral ritual that follows is confirmed against the wiki plot record: the Board selects her as Director on the spot, and she steps out into a House already wearing the Hiss.

The Hiss announces itself with one endlessly repeated sentence:

"You are a worm through time."

The chant is confirmed as the Hiss incantation that Trench himself repeats on the Hotline tapes.

Dylan, found voluntarily contained, warns Jesse against the voice inside her own head:

"Do not trust Polaris."

The warning itself is confirmed in the wiki plot record, while the exact wording here stays unverified outside the video's captions.

The first impression that Trench simply took his own life is corrected by the tapes. The Hiss reached him during Darling's expeditions, and he opened the door himself with a kept burned slide. Jesse's counterstroke, shutting the projector and pulling Dylan back from the Hiss at the cost of his waking mind, is confirmed in the ending breakdown, which also notes the lockdown holds until the remaining Hiss dies.

4. Machinery

Three objects carry the plot. The Service Weapon binds and appoints. The Hotline carries Trench's lingering orders. The Slide Projector moves the invasion from backstory to present tense.

Ahti's cassette player earns its own mission later; it is the only key to the maze guarding the projector's chamber.
