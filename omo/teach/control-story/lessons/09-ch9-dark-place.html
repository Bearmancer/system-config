3. Narrative

The sector was sealed after its AWE work went wrong, and Jesse pries it open following visions of Wake. That setup is confirmed in the AWE review, which names Hartman, consumed by darkness and worsened by Hiss interference, as the horror at its center.

Wake's own warning about his double survives in the coverage:

"He's out there."

The line is confirmed in the AWE story piece, which also confirms the sting: Langston flags the event date as years in the future, grooming Jesse for a Bright Falls trip that has not happened yet.

The crossover status is confirmed at the top: the AWE expansion is the first formal event of the shared universe, per the studio's own statement. Whether Wake authored any of Jesse's story stays unverified, and the coverage treats it as tease rather than fact.

4. Machinery

Light is the combat verb here. Hartman cannot be hurt in darkness, so lamps and flares become weapons and the sector reads as horror rather than shooter.

The Investigations Sector itself is an archive machine: every AWE case Jesse replays is a file the Bureau closed too early.
