3. Narrative

The Bureau's own files date the Oldest House discovery to the nineteen-sixties, and the agency grew up around the building rather than the other way round. That founding order is confirmed on the Bureau page, while exact dates stay unverified in this pass.

Northmoor is the cautionary tale the Bureau keeps locked up: a Director whose paranatural strength outgrew the chair until containment became the only option. The containment itself is confirmed in the canon summaries, and Dylan's later comparison to Northmoor shows the Bureau still measures candidates against him.

Trench's end replays the pattern from inside:

"You are a worm through time."

The chant is confirmed as the Hiss voice Trench carries onto the Hotline tapes, which is why his gunshot opens the game but does not end his influence. The Bureau history chapter of the overview record follows the same chain from discovery through Jesse's appointment.

4. Machinery

Director succession is the Bureau's core machine. The Board appoints, the Service Weapon binds, and the Prime Candidate Program keeps spares.

Every spare the program raised is someone Jesse later has to answer for, which is why the program gets a chapter of its own.
