3. Narrative

Children find a projector in a landfill, slides go in, and an Altered World Event erases the adults. The case name itself is confirmed on the Ordinary AWE page, and the Bureau's arrival, seizing Dylan and the projector while Jesse runs, is confirmed in the plot record.

Jesse's counter-move is the fire:

"She burned the slides."

That she destroyed slides is confirmed. How many existed, how many burned, and how many survived is where the accounts split: the video's tally and the projector page's tally do not match, so this lesson repeats the split and leaves it unverified rather than crowning a winner.

One point survives the dispute intact and is confirmed: at least one burned slide reached Trench's hands, and that slide became the Hiss road in.

4. Machinery

Slides are addresses. Each one points the projector at a dimension, and Slidescape-36 is the address everything later depends on.

Burning a slide closes its address, which is why the surviving burned slide matters more than the destroyed ones.
