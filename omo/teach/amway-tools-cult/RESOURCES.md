## Knowledge — documents & records
- **FTC, *In re Amway Corp.*, 93 F.T.C. 618 — complaint 1975, final order 8 May 1979** **[D]**
  The administrative decision that drew the line between Amway and an illegal pyramid scheme ("the Amway safeguards": 70% rule, retail sales rule, buy-back rule). *Not* the source of the Wisconsin income figures. Full volume: https://www.ftc.gov/sites/default/files/documents/commission_decision_volumes/volume-93/ftc_volume_decision_93_january_-_june_1979pages_618-738.pdf · Use for: regulation claims in later chapters.
- ***State of Wisconsin v. Amway Corp. et al.*, Dane County #589806, filed July 1982** **[D]**
  Asst AG Bruce Craig's subpoena of Wisconsin Direct Distributor tax returns for 1979–1980; expert affidavit: average net income a **loss of $918** (~200 Directs of ~20,000 distributorships). Affidavit PDF: https://pyramidscheme.law/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/buseaff001.pdf · Case references: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dst/Amway/AUS/refs.htm · Use for: the distributor-income claim in chapter 2 and any later chapter that reuses it.
- ***60 Minutes* segment on Amway (as quoted in Fitzpatrick, 1980s)** **[D]**
  Craig on camera: "After business expenses, a net income of minus $918"; the "$14,000 gross … losing almost $1000" exchange with Mike Wallace. Quoted transcript: https://www.pyramidschemealert.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/WhatIsthisThingCalledMLM.pdf · Use for: the dated, on-record version of the Wisconsin figure.
