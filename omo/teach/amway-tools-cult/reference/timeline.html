How to read this page

Every entry is one date, one line of event, one colour tag for its kind, and a status in plain words. Verified means the date/event was checked against the record in a lesson or cited source (the note says which). Source-claimed means it rests on the video's account alone — asserted, no record located. Colour carries kind only, never status. Where the record corrects the video, the entry sits at the corrected date and says so.

Legend — six kinds, one colour each

  • founding & business companies founded, people joining, products, sales, fortunes
  • regulation & courts FTC complaints, decisions, lawsuits, prosecutions
  • media & publication books, records, tapes-as-publications, press exposés
  • politics presidents, resignations, rallies with candidates, attempts
  • internal Amway documents & actions memos, studies, tapes, terminations by the corporation
  • meetings & confrontations founding scenes, showdowns, rallies, parallel meetings

Status is written out, every entry: "verified — …" (with the source) or "source-claimed — …" (why it is not verified). Kind meanings stay stable as the page grows; old entries are never recoloured.

Act I — The raw material, 1936–1963

  • 1936
    media & publication Dale Carnegie publishes How to Win Friends and Influence People. Verified — publisher record / Britannica; lesson 02 check.
  • 1937
    media & publication Napoleon Hill publishes Think and Grow Rich, later atop the Amway reading list. Verified — publisher record / Britannica; lesson 02 check; the "con man" portrait is investigative journalism, not peer review.
  • 1939, Dec 6
    founding & business Dexter Yager born in Fulton, New York; raised in Rome. Verified — obituaries; correction: the video says "born in Rome", conflating birthplace with hometown.
  • 1945
    founding & business Mytinger & Casselberry become Nutrilite's national distributors — the multi-level selling mechanism is invented. Verified — Branchik scholarship; correction: "first MLM" is a 1945 mechanism, not a 1930s company.
  • 1946
    founding & business Doug Wead born in Indiana; lifelong Pentecostal, later Yager's ghostwriter and a tools kingpin. Verified in part — 1946, Indiana confirmed (day cited 17 May, one mirror 19 May — unresolved); the Pentecostal background is confirmed; the ghostwriter/kingpin roles stay the video's account.
  • 1949
    founding & business DeVos and Van Andel form Ja-Ri Corporation, their Nutrilite distributorship vehicle. Verified — corporate history; correction: the video's "Amway founded in 1949" compresses Ja-Ri into Amway (1959).
  • 1954
    meetings & confrontations The Fontainebleau Hotel opens on Miami Beach (grand opening 20 December) — site of both the founding scene (~1970–71) and the 1983 showdown. Verified — the hotel's own history (grand opening 20 Dec 1954); correction: the video says 1955.
  • 1956
    media & publication Earl Nightingale releases the spoken-word record The Strangest Secret. Verified — Discogs / Nightingale-Conant for the 1956 record; lesson 02 check; the "first Gold Record" claim stays soft (traces to his own company's marketing).
  • 1957
    media & publication Charles Manson enrols in the Dale Carnegie course at Terminal Island — and drops out before completing it (Guinn; Telegraph/SMH 2017). Verified in part — Guinn (Bloomberg 2013) for the enrolment and the technique's later use; the completion claim is wrong — he dropped out.
  • 1959 (Apr; commonly Nov 9)
    founding & business The American Way Association is incorporated; Amway Sales & Services follow — the founding of Amway. Verified — corporate history / IBOAI timeline.

Act II — Amway and the parallel empires, 1964–1969

  • 1964
    founding & business Yager joins Amway; Ja-Ri is absorbed into Amway Corporation; William Penn Patrick founds Holiday Magic on the Amway model (founded 14 Oct 1964; NYT, Jun 1973). Verified — obituaries/corporate history for the Amway half; video's account, uncontested, for Holiday Magic's founding year.
  • ~1966
    founding & business Someone — possibly Fred Hanson — lends Yager Nightingale's Strangest Secret record. Source-claimed — the Hanson loan is unverified, and the record says the Basses, not DeVos, sponsored Hanson. Spelling per Wead's 2009 blog post: Hanson, not the video's Hansen.
  • 1966, fall
    founding & business Yager sponsors Tony Renard into Amway; Renard buys a second-hand tape recorder and begins duplicating motivational tapes. Verified in part — resolved to Wead's 2009 blog post (not The Magic Makers): Renard was a piano player in Schenectady who bought a reel-to-reel from Dexter for $46.75; wife Sue. The 1966 sponsorship half stays the video's account.
  • ~1967
    founding & business Yager "applying for other jobs" within three years of joining — the video's evidence the model was already exhausted. Source-claimed — asserted, no record located; the video's inference.
  • 1967
    founding & business Patrick founds Leadership Dynamics, pioneering large-group awareness training. Verified — lesson 02 parallels check (LGAT scholarship; Mind Dynamics).
  • 1968
    founding & business Alexander Everett founds Mind Dynamics; Patrick later holds a stake — buy-in dated 1970 in some sources, 1968 in others — unresolved. Verified — lesson 02 check (LGAT scholarship; Mind Dynamics).
  • 1969
    founding & business The Yagers move Rome → Charlotte, NC; Glenn Turner founds Dare to Be Great! in Orlando — graduated cassette courses totalling $5,000 for all four adventures. Verified — obituaries for the move; NYT, 13 Jan 1972 for the pricing; correction: $5,000, not $8,000.

Act III — The tools business is born, 1970–1974

  • 1970
    founding & business Bill Britt joins Amway (sponsor: Dominic Conigliaro, beneath Yager); ex-Army officer, municipal manager in central North Carolina. Verified — obituary; correction: central NC (Raleigh/Sanford/Goldsboro/Carrboro), not "the Charlotte area".
  • ~1970–71
    meetings & confrontations The Fontainebleau coffee-shop pitch: a nameless young man offers a $50,000 tape plant; Yager declines, Britt says "I'll do it." Source-claimed — Wead's account only; the exact quote's true source is Wead's 2009 blog post, not The Magic Makers (1982; controlled-lending IA id magicmakershowto00yage).
  • 1971, Jan 18
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Holiday Magic (Docket 8834). Verified — 84 F.T.C. 748.
  • 1972, May 24
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Koscot (Docket 8888). Verified — 86 F.T.C. 1106.
  • 1972
    media & publication A book-length exposé of Mind Dynamics appears. Verified — Church & Carnes, The Pit: A Group Encounter Defiled (Outerbridge & Lazard, 1972).
  • 1973
    politics Vice President Spiro Agnew resigns in a bribery scandal (10 Oct 1973); Gerald Ford succeeds him under the 25th Amendment, becoming VP in Dec 1973. Verified — public record.
  • 1973
    founding & business William Penn Patrick dies in a P-51 crash (9 Jun 1973). Verified — NYT, 10 Jun 1973; correction: Leadership Dynamics / Mind Dynamics shut in 1973, Holiday Magic dissolved 1974.
  • 1973
    regulation & courts F. Lee Bailey and Glenn Turner indicted on Koscot conspiracy charges (18 May 1973, 28 counts mail fraud + conspiracy); trial began 17 Sept 1973; Bailey severed 25 Apr 1974; mistrial for the others 30 May 1974 when the jury could not agree; charges then dropped against Bailey; Turner pleaded guilty to a single securities misdemeanor in 1975. Verified — United States v. Bailey, M.D. Fla. 1975 opinion (vlex); NYT "Glenn Turner and F. Lee Bailey Indicted on Charges of Fraud" (May 1973).
  • 1974, Aug
    politics Gerald Ford becomes president after Watergate — the Grand Rapids congressman next door to Ada is in the White House. Verified — public record.
  • 1974, Oct 15
    regulation & courts FTC decision against Holiday Magic: cease the "open-ended, multilevel marketing plan". Verified — 84 F.T.C. 748.

Act IV — The FTC years, 1975–1979

  • 1975, Mar 25
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Amway (Docket 9023) — the four-year battle begins. Verified — FTC docket.
  • 1975
    politics DeVos and Van Andel "visited [Ford] privately in the White House" — content unrecorded, deal (if any) unknown. Source-claimed — asserted, no record of the conversation located; the wider Ford–Amway tie is documented and the Ford papers do hold Amway-related material (1965 convention speech; Aug 1975 White House press file) — the Daily Diaries are the unsearched-by-us place a visit would appear; the meeting's content is unknown.
  • 1975, Sep 5
    politics Lynette "Squeaky" Fromme attempts to shoot President Ford in Sacramento. Verified — Ford Library blog for the attempt; correction: no source ties Fromme's own recruitment to Carnegie technique (Manson's was).
  • 1975, Nov
    regulation & courts Koscot final order — driven out of business as an illegal pyramid. Verified — 86 F.T.C. 1106 (month firm; the volume's OCR gives an unreliable day).
  • 1978
    media & publication Don't Let Anybody Steal Your Dream appears under Yager's byline — probably ghostwritten by Wead. Source-claimed — the video's attribution; the book's emptiness is the video's judgment.
  • 1978, Nov
    media & publication Jonestown: 909 dead in Guyana (914 with Georgetown) — the cult Kerns and Wead will co-write about. Verified — NYT, Nov 1978.
  • 1979
    media & publication Kerns and Wead publish Peoples Temple, Peoples Tomb (Logos). Verified — Open Library / SDSU Jonestown archive.
  • 1979, May 8
    regulation & courts In re Amway Corp. final order: not an illegal pyramid — but also guilty of resale-price maintenance and misleading earnings claims. Verified — 93 F.T.C. 618; correction: a limited order with operating rules, not a ringing endorsement or statutory safe harbor.
  • 1979–80 (video says "1980")
    regulation & courts Wisconsin Direct Distributors' tax returns average a net loss of $918 after business expenses. Verified — expert affidavit; correction: $918 not $900, tax years 1979–80, case filed July 1982; "almost every single one" is the video's embellishment (only the average is sourced).

Act V — The apex, 1980–1981

  • 1980, late summer
    politics Candidate Ronald Reagan speaks at a massive Yager rally in Charlotte; Wead's quickie Reagan biography is dedicated to Yager. Verified in substance — Phoenix New Times corroborates the Amway-rally setting, Wead's introduction and the Yager dedication; the "late summer" dating stays the video's.
  • 1980
    meetings & confrontations Free Enterprise Days: a Charlotte rally spills into the streets at 3 a.m. chanting "Five and six nights a week." Verified in part — chant on record (Detroit Free Press, 1982); the rally staging is the video's account.
  • 1980
    founding & business Yager's wealth portrait at its peak: the Lake Wylie mansion as pilgrimage site, Rolls-Royces, boats, jet, the "Freedom Special" motor home. Verified in substance, details adjusted — log house on Lake Wylie; 3 Rolls + 4 Mercedes; 4 boats + Hawker jet; $350k motor home. Verified in substance against the Observer's 1995 series via an archive mirror — treat the host as advocacy-adjacent; verify against the paywalled original where possible.
  • 1980
    founding & business Freedom Distributing formed — the financial hub of Yager's tools empire (Internet Services Corp. from 1989; "50 states and 10 foreign countries"). Verified — Observer series; Fifth Circuit (sale/asset transfer); the exact day is not in the video's slice.
  • 1980
    founding & business Amway's $1 billion sales year. Verified — Amway's own corporate chronicle; caution against reading 1982 as a revenue collapse.
  • ~1981
    founding & business Wead becomes an Amway distributor himself. Source-claimed — the video's "on or around 1981" dating.

Act VI — The battle, 1982–April 1983

  • 1982
    media & publication Phil Kerns publishes Fake It 'Til You Make It (Victory Press) — "the first book-length exposé" (a critic's assessment). Verified — Open Library; "first" qualified, not established fact.
  • 1982, Jul
    regulation & courts State of Wisconsin v. Amway Corp. filed (Dane County #589806); AG Craig subpoenas Directs' returns. Verified — case references; correction: filed July 1982, not "1980".
  • 1982, Aug 26
    media & publication NYT: "U.S. Disputes Amway Claims" — press heat in the terrible year. Verified — NYT archive.
  • 1982, late
    internal Amway documents & actions Amway commissions a comprehensive internal study of the tools business after the crosslining affair. Source-claimed — named but not detailed in the lessons; the January memo is consistent with, but does not prove, a commissioned study.
  • 1982, late
    internal Amway documents & actions Leonard Hall poaches distributors up and down the California coast with his own tools line — crosslining; Amway terminates his distributorship. Verified in part — the video admits it knows "very little"; one independent trace: the archived Amquix attorneys page records "a crown distributorship, Leonard Hall in California was selling his system to the distributors who had no system at the time… in the early 1980's". No press or court trace (a distributor wiki conflicts on dates).
  • 1982
    internal Amway documents & actions The Challenge of the 80s warns the Policy Committee the tools' illegality threatens Amway's existence. Verified in part — existence reported (Amway Policy Committee memo, Aug 1982; excerpts in exhibits and press); full text still not public.
  • 1982
    internal Amway documents & actions Internal Amway Sales Division memo, "Amway Distributor Compliance with the Code of Ethics and Rules of Conduct" (author/date unknown, probably 1982), later entered as a plaintiff's exhibit in Cairns v. Amway (Cincinnati, 1984): "Widespread illegalities inherent in Amway distributor designed 'systems' of tapes, books, and rallies." Verified — memo text via the CMU archive's Cairns exhibits (critic-hosted; the video calls it a court warning — correction).
  • 1983, Jan 10
    internal Amway documents & actions Ed Postma (business conduct) to Patric Sullivan (Northwest sales): the Britt/Yager analysis — "The tool business is illegal… It is a pyramid." Verified — memo text via the CMU archive scan (critic-hosted); header: "TO: Patric Sullivan [sic in the memo] / FROM: Ed Postma / DATE: January 10, 1983 / SUBJECT: ANALYSIS OF THE BRITT/YAGER SYSTEM". Discrepancy kept visible, not harmonised: the archived Amquix "Dateline" analysis page dates the Postma analysis 19 Jan 1983.
  • 1983, Jan
    internal Amway documents & actions Rich DeVos alone records the two Directly Speaking tapes (Series VA-2160 / VAL-2150, anonymised) — BV on tapes at $2.50, no PV, BV capped at 20% of total Business Volume; count Amway-approved tapes toward bonuses; the kingpins read it as a declaration of war. Verified in substance — Cairns exhibit transcripts (critic-hosted); correction: DeVos alone, not DeVos and Van Andel.
  • 1983, early
    media & publication 60 Minutes airs "Soap and Hope": Mike Wallace interviews Dexter and Birdie Yager, leaves the tools-vs-Amway income question open. Verified in part — broadcast and the Yager/DeVos interviews confirmed (MLive; scholarship cites 9 Jan 1983); the video's reading of what the segment leaves open stays the video's account.
  • ~1983, early
    meetings & confrontations A big confrontation meeting in Hawaii — mentioned in several sources including the Miami tape; no record survives. Source-claimed — asserted, no record located for the confrontation; an Amway Diamond Club in Hawaii (early 1983) is documented in the Directly Speaking transcript, but the kingpins-versus-suits meeting the video describes is not; the video says it is lost to history.
  • 1983, Apr 5
    meetings & confrontations The meeting the video calls the "Miami Showdown" — a name Munger says he coined, not an insider term: Ron Lindebom (captions garble; not Lindebaum) and Bill (William) Abraham — outside counsel for distributors, brother of Rick J. Abraham, Abraham Law Offices, Columbus OH — meet the kingpins (Herrera, Hale, Wead, Dexter and Birdie Yager, others) on a Fontainebleau terrace; taped, acrimonious, cordial at the close. Verified in part — provenance resolved: first published on amquix.info, June 2003 (operator Scott Larsen); archived page lists dc_1983_*.zip, ec_1983_*.zip, cut clips. The source page calls the recording location a rooftop where the video says "terrace" — wording difference noted. No archive.org item exists; the audio survives as Wayback captures. No attendee roster; neither Amway name is confirmable in the record.
  • 1983, Apr 5
    meetings & confrontations Same day, same hotel: a scripted ballroom session with the lower-level "emeralds" — fewer revelations, tape incomplete. Verified in part — the second meeting the same evening is confirmed; its files are named in the audio index (ec_1983_1.zip, ec_1983_2.zip, "better organized and clearly recorded"). "Ballroom" is the video's word for the venue.

Act VII — The decline and the surrender, 1983–1988

  • 1983, Nov 10
    regulation & courts Amway of Canada pleads guilty over 1965–80 customs undervaluation; fined $25m. Verified — contemporaneous reporting; lesson 05 check. Amway conceded the case "has not helped our business."
  • 1983, Nov 19–21
    founding & business Amway's FY1983 decline goes public: revenues down $70m to $1.13bn (NYT, 21 Nov 1983); 230 layoffs (UPI, 19 Nov 1983); the company blames the economy and the strong dollar. Verified — NYT/UPI via lesson 05 check. Caution: read 1982 as the base year, not as a multi-year collapse; the critic mirror's $1.5bn figure is not the base.
  • 1984
    regulation & courts Cairns et al. v. Amway, S.D. Ohio: 79 distributors from five states sue over coerced motivational-materials spending; settled out of court. Verified — lesson 05 check; correction to the video's sorting: the 1980s suits protest forced tools spending, not break-in bids (those belong to the 1990s–2000s).
  • 1986
    regulation & courts FTC consent decree: $100,000 over 1983 newspaper ads violating the earnings-disclosure order (monthly income $67; Ruby Bonus $288 average). Verified — lesson 05 check; the only pre-1990 federal follow-up found.
  • 1986, Jul 17
    regulation & courts Rick Setzer and Sue Lynn Setzer v. Amway Corp., Richard M. DeVos and Jay Van Andel filed, Greenville, SC (6:86-1898-3); plaintiff a Triple Diamond suing over interference with his tools business. Verified — docket via lesson 05 check; the video says the case is unclear from its sources.
  • 1987, Mar 19
    politics Jim Bakker resigns as head of the PTL ministry over the payoff to church secretary Jessica Hahn; hides for a time in the guesthouse on Yager's estate. Verified — lesson 06 check (thesis + press); convicted of fraud and conspiracy 1989; paroled 1994.
  • 1988, Apr
    meetings & confrontations Rich DeVos on the stand in the Setzer trial: the tools abuses addressed at Miami "continued to this day"; the correction cost "upward of $300 million in volume"; "we never pursued it to its ultimate goal of really nailing anything down." Verified — sworn Q&A verbatim from the critic-hosted transcript excerpt; lesson 05 check. The $300m is DeVos's sworn estimate, not an audited loss.
  • 1988, Nov
    politics George H. W. Bush elected president, "with generous help of Doug Wead and numerous Amway distributors" (the video's crediting). Verified in part — Wead as liaison to the Christian right confirmed (Bush 41 Library; PBS Frontline); no count of distributors found — "numerous" stays the video's.
