Covers chapters 5–12 · verified against the record vs the source telling

How to read

Each entry states its status in text. Verified means confirmed against the cited record in the lessons. Source-claimed means it rests on the video telling alone. Corrected means the video date or figure was fixed — the lesson carries the record version. Colour tags mark kind; text alone carries full coverage.
