Watergate: A Geographic History

Sean Munger · 12 chapters · this course covers chapters 5–12

A geographic telling of Watergate: the back channel and the leak, the covert unit and the cover-up, the tapes and the scandal, the resignation and its aftermath. Eight lessons below follow the source chapters in order.

Lessons — chapters 5–12

  1. Lesson 01 — The Roots of Watergate (chapter 5) — the Chennault back channel, the Pentagon Papers arc, the hidden machinery.
  2. Lesson 02 — The Plumbers (chapter 6) — the Special Investigations Unit, Brookings to Fielding to the Watergate.
  3. Lesson 03 — The Cover-Up (chapter 7) — why confession never came; Huston Plan, June 19 and June 23 orders.
  4. Lesson 04 — The Tapes (chapter 8) — the taping system, three decisive reels, disclosure, the custody fight.
  5. Lesson 05 — The Scandal (chapter 9) — landslide, garage source, four investigations, Massacre, Agnew to Ford.
  6. Lesson 06 — Resignation (chapter 10) — impeachment clock, unanimous Court, smoking-gun tape, August 9.
  7. Lesson 07 — The Aftermath (chapter 11) — prison, pardon, Frost interviews, exile to Yorba Linda.
  8. Lesson 08 — Conclusion (chapter 12) — site layers, wide map, connections thesis.
