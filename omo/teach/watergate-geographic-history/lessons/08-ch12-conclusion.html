4. The machinery

The mechanism has two moves. Layering puts the scandal late: each earlier use of the ground makes the break-in look smaller without making it matter less. Linking puts the scandal wide: each far place named becomes a spoke back to the same hub. Layering handles time, linking handles space, and the thesis sentence binds them into one claim about what history is.
