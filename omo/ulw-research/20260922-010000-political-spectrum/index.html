Where Left and Right Come From

Deputies sat left for revolution and right for monarchy in Versailles in 1789 (Britannica).

Baron de Gauville wrote loyalists “took up positions to the right of the chair,” confirmed against record.B

Left became the party of movement. Right became the party of order.

Labels took decades to harden. Full spread needed into the early 1900s.T

One axis misleads. Add authority: libertarian versus authoritarian (Political Spectrum).

Neoliberal Politics, Neoclassical Analysis

Neoliberalism reorganizes state power to expand markets (Naked Keynesianism).

Its rhetoric cites Smith. Its math belongs to marginalism, verified against source wording.N

All neoliberals use neoclassical logic. Not all neoclassicals are neoliberal.

Mont Pelerin Society formed in 1947. Lippmann colloquium met in 1938 (Taylor and Francis).

Chicago gave the program teeth: monetarism, deregulation, market primacy (Neoliberalism).

Williamson codified ten rules in 1989 for IMF and World Bank loans.W

Progressivism as American Middle Way

Progressivism seeks progress beyond status quo toward just conditions (Center for American Progress).

It rose against industrial misery between 1890 and 1920.C

It steered between European socialism and hands-off conservatism.

Line runs Era to New Deal to Great Society to Green New Deal.

Modern faces include Sanders, Warren, Ocasio-Cortez, Mamdani (Britannica 21st Century).

Warren brands as capitalist patriot. Sanders claims democratic socialist internationalism (Council on Foreign Relations).

Socialism, in Three Boxes

Social democracy keeps capitalism and adds safety nets (Good Party).

Democratic socialism seeks worker and community ownership instead.G

Goal differs: reform versus transform.

Nordic model is mixed market plus universal welfare (Nordic Model).

Sweden grew fastest before welfare arrived. Markets still drive Nordics.M

DSA, the American Variant

DSA merged in 1982 and stayed multi-tendency (DSA History).

It held near 5,000 for decades. Then Sanders 2016 pushed it near 30,000 by 2017.

It peaked near 79,000 in 2021. It fell to 51,000 by October 2024.D

Trump return plus Mamdani lifted it to 92,912 by December 2025 (City and State).

It runs candidates on Democratic ballot to build independent power.

Over 250 members hold office across 40 states.

How America Votes Now

Gallup 2024 reads 37% conservative, 34% moderate, 25% liberal.

Independents are largest bloc. Peak hit 51% in June 2024.

College once predicted GOP. It now predicts Democrats.

GOP is 77% conservative. Democrats are 55% liberal. Both records.
