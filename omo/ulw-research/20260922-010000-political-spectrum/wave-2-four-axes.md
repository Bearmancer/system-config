# Wave 2 — baseline / neoliberal / progressive / socialism / DSA (direct saturation 2026-09-22)

## Baseline left-right-center
- Origin 1789 French National Assembly Versailles. Left = revolution egalitarian movement, right = monarchy tradition hierarchy, center undecided. Sources: Wikipedia Left-right spectrum, Britannica political spectrum, TIME Rosenfeld/Higonnet, Gauchet long process to early 1900s.
- Modern: left prioritize equality, right prioritize hierarchy. Intermediate = centrist moderate. Two-axis extensions: radicalism-conservatism + tender/tough authoritarian-libertarian. Liberalism situational left or right by context.
- Center ever-changing situational. US today left=progressivism right=conservatism.

## Neoliberal vs neoclassical
- Neoliberalism = post-war political movement Mont Pelerin Society 1947, Lippmann 1938, take over state to expand markets protect capital. Economics marginalist market-efficiency. All neoliberals neoclassical analytically, not all neoclassicals neoliberal. Source: Naked Keynesianism Vernengo/Mirowski.
- Neoclassical = school analysis marginalism market efficiency. Many emphasize market failure accept public policy.
- Chicago School = neoclassical engine: Friedman Stigler Coase Becker Hayek, monetarism rational expectations, shift market failure to government failure, deregulation privatization primacy markets.
- Washington Consensus 1989 Williamson 10 rules IMF World Bank Treasury: liberalize trade control inflation privatize cut deficits. Structural adjustment Latin America. Not synonym neoliberalism originally but became shorthand.
- Timeline: Austrian Freiburg Chicago early 1900s, Hayek MPS, 1970s consensus, Thatcher-Reagan 1980s, structural adjustment, 1990s knowledge economy.

## Socialism / democratic socialism / social democracy / Nordic
- Socialism = shared public ownership means production.
- Social democracy = capitalist economy + private sector + regulation safety net reform system. Works within Dems. Goal reform.
- Democratic socialism = replace capitalism cooperative worker community ownership transform system. Works via DSA grassroots independent third-party. Goal transform.
- Blurred colloquially. DSA definition broad leans social democracy in practice confusion source.
- Social democracy = most common Western modern socialism reformist wing, universalist welfare collective bargaining public provision, Nordic model.
- Nordic = Denmark Finland Iceland Norway Sweden market mixed economy private ownership collective bargaining grand compromise 1930s farmer worker parties. Prospered 1870-1970 Sweden fastest ex Japan before socialists. High economic freedom ease business low regulation. Welfare large but not socialist ownership. Norway exception state enterprises oil fund. Mises critique: proof free market works.
